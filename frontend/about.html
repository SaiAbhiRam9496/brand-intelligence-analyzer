About the Platform

The Brand Intelligence Analyzer is a modern multi-tier analytics system designed to track brand health, customer sentiment, and strategic market placement.

By scraping text data from multiple public social pipelines (News articles, YouTube comment sections, Google Play Store app reviews, and Reddit discussions), our NLP engine processes text to output brand sentiment ratings, topic categorizations, and intent classifications.

Multi-Pipeline Collection

Integrates multiple search nodes into one cohesive data stack, automatically filtering out noise and non-English text.

Dynamic Topic Modeling

Applies BERTopic with automated clustering adjustments to accurately summarize customer pain points.

Zero-Shot Tone Alignment

Analyzes target brand output against consumer opinion to detect presentation gaps.

Strategic Groq Reports

Leverages Llama 3 models to suggest practical product and marketing pivots.
