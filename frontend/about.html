About the Platform

The Brand Intelligence Analyzer is a product we built to solve a critical problem for businesses: understanding exactly what customers are complaining about and knowing how to fix it instantly.

Instead of just showing raw sentiment scores, our product actively analyzes massive amounts of public feedback, intelligently groups identical problems together into distinct clusters, and leverages AI to recommend highly specific, actionable solutions to those exact problems. It turns raw market noise into a clear, strategic action plan.

Multi-Pipeline Collection

Integrates multiple search nodes into one cohesive data stack, automatically filtering out noise and non-English text.

Dynamic Topic Modeling

Applies BERTopic with automated clustering adjustments to accurately summarize customer pain points.

Zero-Shot Tone Alignment

Analyzes target brand output against consumer opinion to detect presentation gaps.

Strategic Groq Reports

Leverages Llama 3 models to suggest practical product and marketing pivots.
